Who I am

I am a curious engineer who loves to learn and engage with a bit of everything. I am completing a Master's in Computer Engineering — Mobile Computing (graduating July 2026) and work at Glartek as a Software Quality Engineer.

I have delivered AR digital twins and immersive VR for real clients, explored cybersecurity, and co-founded a community cooperative — and I am just as invested in the people, communities, and causes I commit to outside of work. What stays constant is how I like to work: understanding a problem properly, building something honest, and leaving things better than I found them. This site is my attempt to lay all of that out plainly.
